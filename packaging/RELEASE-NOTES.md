## 🧚 Faery Tale Adventure II: Community Edition

**Development alpha.** A full playthrough and complete translation review remain pending.

### 📥 Downloads

- **Windows x64:** `fta2-ce-VERSION-Windows-x86_64.zip`
- **Linux x64:** `fta2-ce-VERSION-Linux-x86_64.zip`
- **macOS Apple Silicon and Intel:** `fta2-ce-VERSION-Darwin-universal.zip`

Extract the entire ZIP and open its root launcher. Linux/macOS users may need to
allow execution in their file manager or operating-system security settings.
Each ZIP includes verified game data under `data/`, original English voices under
`media/`, SDL, required licences and the matching engine source ZIP under `source/`.
Verify your download against `SHA256SUMS`.

### 💾 Ready to play

Extract the complete ZIP and open its root launcher. Game data and English voices
are detected automatically; no separate installation or folder selection is
needed. Saves, settings and logs go to your user profile. No original saves are
included. Original game content and recordings retain their copyright and are
not relicensed under the engine GPL. Source builds and desktop CI artifacts
remain engine-only.

### 🌍 Languages and versioning

English, Czech, Spanish and French are available. Translated story text still
includes drafts. Original voices remain English.

Community Edition numbering restarts at **0.0.1**. Earlier 0.1.x builds are
historical development snapshots; the reset does not remove features or saves.
